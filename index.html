    Recording & transcription

    Choose your capture mode

    Information about recording and transcription options

    Dictation uses your browser's built-in transcription and works well for a single voice or when everyone is in the same room. Browser transcription is free of charge. Record audio uses OpenAI transcription models and is usually safer for more complex audio, such as speaker playback plus people in the room. OpenAI transcription uses your API key and comes with a cost. Both add text to the Live transcript field.

    Audio transcription uses gpt-4o-mini-transcribe.

    No audio file selected yet. You can record here or upload mp3, m4a, wav, mp4, or webm.

    Model

    Choose an OpenAI model

    Loading model guidance and pricing...

    Pricing is shown per 1 million tokens. In plain language, tokens are small chunks of text, so this is a standardized way to compare cost across models. For normal meeting notes, a single polish pass usually uses only a tiny fraction of that amount. As a rough guide, one page of text is about 500 words, which is approximately 750 tokens.
